# Shared parse() cases, used by test_python.py (correctness) and
# test_parity.py (C++ / Python / Jsonnet give identical results).
#
# Format:  expression | symbols | expected
#   symbols:  name=value;name=value   (may be empty)
#   expected: a Python expression over the hep_units module `u`,
#             or "!error" when parse() must reject the expression.
#
# --- the original flat product/quotient grammar ---
1.5*cm                ||  1.5*u.cm
1.5 cm                ||  1.5*u.cm
cm                    ||  u.cm
2*pi                  ||  2*u.pi
eplus                 ||  1.0
9.8 m/s^2             ||  9.8*u.m/(u.s*u.s)
1.6 mm/us             ||  1.6*u.mm/u.us
3 mm*ns               ||  3*u.mm*u.ns
1/s                   ||  1/u.s
1/s s                 ||  1/u.s*u.s
2**3                  ||  8.0
# --- arithmetic needed by GDML attributes ---
0.5*0.02              ||  0.5*0.02
2.3/2                 ||  2.3/2
(1+2)*mm              ||  3*u.mm
2 cm + 3 mm           ||  2*u.cm + 3*u.mm
2 - 3                 ||  -1.0
-1.5 cm               ||  -1.5*u.cm
+4                    ||  4.0
2*(3-5)/4             ||  2*(3-5)/4
(2)(3)                ||  6.0
2(3)                  ||  6.0
500*V/cm             ||  !error
# (V is not a CLHEP/hep-units symbol; GDML Efield auxvalues use it.)
500*volt/cm           ||  500*u.volt/u.cm
# --- numbers ---
1e-3*m                ||  1e-3*u.m
1.5E+2 mm             ||  150.0
1.                    ||  1.0
.5                    ||  0.5
1.e3                  ||  1000.0
007                   ||  7.0
# --- powers ---
s^-1                  ||  1/u.s
-2^2                  ||  -4.0
2^3^2                 ||  512.0
2^-2                  ||  0.25
# --- caller symbols (e.g. GDML <constant>s) ---
0.5*kWireDiam         | kWireDiam=0.015 | 0.5*0.015
kTPCWidth/2 + 1       | kTPCWidth=10 | 6.0
EWProfileLength*cm    | EWProfileLength=2.5 | 2.5*u.cm
m                     | m=5 | 5.0
a*b                   | a=2;b=3 | 6.0
# --- functions ---
sqrt(2)*cm            ||  2**0.5*u.cm
pow(2, 10)            ||  1024.0
min(1, 2) + max(1, 2) ||  3.0
abs(-3)               ||  3.0
cos(0)                ||  1.0
exp(0)                ||  1.0
log(1)                ||  0.0
atan(1)*4             ||  3.141592653589793
# --- errors ---
                      ||  !error
1 +                   ||  !error
(1                    ||  !error
1)                    ||  !error
furlong               ||  !error
foo(1)                ||  !error
sqrt(1, 2)            ||  !error
1 $ 2                 ||  !error
.                     ||  !error
